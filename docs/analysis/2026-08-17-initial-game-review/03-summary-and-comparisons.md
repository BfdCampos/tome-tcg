# Summary & comparisons

## The game's identity

It's a **duel of simultaneous double-rock-paper-scissors, layered on a
"spell-stack" engine.** The genius/risk of it is that **you can't just play the
strongest card — you have to guess your opponent's colour.** A 40-attack turn does
nothing if they picked the colour that beats you. That's fundamentally different
from the "biggest board / most resources wins" logic of most TCGs. The stacking
system gives it a slow-burn engine-building layer (Overgrown Root scaling,
Orb-on-Orb, "top card is active") on top of the bluffing.

## Same / unique vs each game

| Game | What Tome shares | What's genuinely different |
|---|---|---|
| **Yu-Gi-Oh** | Reactive triggers, on-reveal/on-damage effects, spell/field distinction | No summoning/tribute, no chains/priority, no monster board — combat is colour-RPS, not stat-vs-stat. Vastly lower complexity. |
| **Magic** | Colour identity, stack-of-effects hooks, field/enchantment analog | No mana/lands, no tapping, no instants at opponent's turn. One card/turn hard cap. Colours are an RPS wheel, not a resource identity. |
| **Pokémon (TCG)** | Type weakness wheel; HP-as-life; heal | No energy attachment, no bench/evolution, no prizes. Weakness *is* the whole combat system, not a modifier. |
| **Pokémon Pocket** | The **target form factor**: vertical, one-thumb, 3-lane, fast matches, small decks | Pocket is still Pokémon rules (energy, bench, evolve). Tome is a bluff/RPS game — mechanically unrelated, only the *form factor* matches. |
| **Lorcana** | Simple, readable, colour ("ink") identity, approachable | Lorcana races to 20 lore (objective), has quest/challenge choices and inkwell economy. Tome has no alternate win-con or resource curve. |
| **Flesh and Blood** | Combat as **attack vs reaction with a winner**, hand-as-resource tension, no mana ramp | FAB is a physical combat-chain with blocking math; Tome resolves via colour wheel and only the winner connects. Both punish "just play big things." |

## Closest relatives (from online research)

Nothing matches the exact combination. The nearest **conceptual** cousins:

- **Yomi / BattleCON** — simultaneous reveal + rock-paper-scissors combat (but
  fighting-game themed, no colour stacks).
- **Duel Masters / "Earthcore"** — coloured RPS clashes.
- **SolForge** — cards stack/level in lanes over the game.

The specific fusion — **colour-wheel RPS on both a field layer AND an attack
layer, with persistent per-colour spell stacks where only the top card is live** —
was not found anywhere. That's the original core.
